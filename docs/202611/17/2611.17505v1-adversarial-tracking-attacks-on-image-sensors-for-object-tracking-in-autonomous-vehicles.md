## 摘要
目标跟踪系统通过跨视频帧关联检测到的目标来估计周围车辆的运动。特别是，基于检测的跟踪（Tracking-By-Detection，TBD）系统将目标检测器与运动预测和数据关联相结合，以随时间保持目标身份。然而，基于神经网络的检测器易受扰动输入图像的对抗攻击影响，且其对下游轨迹估计的影响不能仅由检测精度充分刻画。因此，本文评估了针对YOLOv3和YOLOv4检测器（分别与SORT、Deep SORT、Strong SORT和OC-SORT跟踪系统配对）的图像特定攻击和图像无关攻击。评估使用CARLA模拟器，并通过多目标跟踪准确率（MOTA）、高阶跟踪准确率（HOTA）和识别F1（IDF1）衡量跟踪质量。在图像特定攻击下，基于YOLOv3的跟踪器的HOTA从53.45-56.19%下降到14.85-17.03%，而MOTA在最坏情况下达到-772.60%。基于YOLOv4的跟踪器表现出更强的鲁棒性，但HOTA仍下降至25.06-26.86%。此外，图像无关攻击可以使用单个通用对抗扰动（UAP）降低跟踪准确率，而无需为各帧单独生成扰动。

## Abstract
Object tracking systems estimate the motion of surrounding vehicles by linking detected objects across video frames. In particular, Tracking-By-Detection (TBD) systems combine object detectors with motion prediction and data association to maintain object identities over time. However, neural network-based detectors are vulnerable to adversarial attacks that perturb input images, and their impact on downstream trajectory estimation is not fully characterized by detection accuracy alone. Therefore, this paper evaluates image-specific and image-agnostic attacks on YOLOv3 and YOLOv4 detectors paired with SORT, Deep SORT, Strong SORT, and OC-SORT tracking systems. The evaluation uses CARLA simulator and measures tracking quality through Multiple Object Tracking Accuracy (MOTA), Higher Order Tracking Accuracy (HOTA), and Identification F1 (IDF1). Under image-specific attacks, HOTA for YOLOv3-based trackers declines from 53.45-56.19% to 14.85-17.03%, while MOTA reaches -772.60% in the worst case. YOLOv4-based trackers exhibit greater robustness, while HOTA still declines to 25.06-26.86%. In addition, image-agnostic attacks can reduce the tracking accuracy using a single Universal Adversarial Perturbation (UAP), without generating a separate perturbation for individual frames.
